Patch 26.23 notes

Every Summoner's Rift change with the exact numbers, what they mean, and where each champion stands in this tier list right now.

26.2326.22

Short version

10buffs
3nerfs
1item
  • Clear moves since 26.22: Mel (Mid) up 2.0, Galio (Support) up 1.4, Syndra (Mid) down 1.2 and Master Yi (Jungle) down 1.1. The rest moved too little to be sure.
  • Items: Voltaic Cyclosword nerfed (core for 13 listed champions).
  • Map: The winter version of Summoner's Rift is back until early next year.
  • Voice chat: Team voice chat (push to talk on C, so character info moves to Z) starts in North America and Oceania only.
  • Champ select: When champ select hostaging (someone threatening to ruin the game unless they get their way) is confirmed within 60 seconds of the game starting, a vote to end the game is now offered.
  • Replays: Replay downloads: 50 per day instead of 10 per hour.
  • 3 new skins and 2 chromas.

Everything else

No rune, summoner spell or ranked changes.

Map

The winter version of Summoner's Rift is back until early next year. All older winter skins can be bought in the shop while it lasts.

Voice chat

Team voice chat (push to talk on C, so character info moves to Z) starts in North America and Oceania only. Other regions, EUW included, follow in later patches.

Champ select

When champ select hostaging (someone threatening to ruin the game unless they get their way) is confirmed within 60 seconds of the game starting, a vote to end the game is now offered. Before, the player was only punished afterwards.

Replays

Replay downloads: 50 per day instead of 10 per hour.
