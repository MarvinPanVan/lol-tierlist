Privacy

Last updated: 14 Nov 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no cookies, no tracking across sites. Cloudflare hosts the site and counts visits without cookies. A few of your settings are saved in your own browser and never leave it.

Who runs this site

LoL Tier List is a private, non-commercial fan project. Contact: marvinpanvan on Discord.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Like every web host, Cloudflare has to process your IP address and basic request data (which page, when, which browser) to deliver the pages and to block attacks. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Cloudflare's own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Visitor counts

To see how many people use the site and which pages they open, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not fingerprint you. It counts page views and shows totals such as the referring site, browser type and country, without building a profile of any visitor. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If a content blocker stops it, the site works exactly the same.

Saved in your browser

The site remembers a few choices in your browser's local storage: light or dark mode, the rank and region you picked, tiers or table, and the champions you starred with their tiers at your last visit (for the "since your last visit" note). This data stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is only saved because you used those features. To remove it, clear the site data for lol-tierlist.pages.dev in your browser.

Fonts and images

Fonts and champion portraits are served from this site. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other image host.

Links to other sites

Buttons like "Build on OP.GG" and "All matchups" open other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Match data

Some statistics come from Riot Games' API. The stored match data holds champions, roles, results and bans, with no player names or account IDs.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your personal data, correction, deletion and restriction of processing, and you can object to processing (Art. 15 to 18 and 21). This site stores nothing about you itself, so questions about server logs mostly concern Cloudflare. You can also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77).

Datenschutz

Kurz: keine Konten, keine Werbung, keine Cookies, kein Tracking über andere Seiten. Cloudflare hostet die Seite und zählt Besuche ohne Cookies. Ein paar Einstellungen werden nur in deinem eigenen Browser gespeichert.
